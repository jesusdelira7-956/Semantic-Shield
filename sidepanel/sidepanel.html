COMMON TROUBLESHOOTING TIPS
• Site Broken or Won't Load? Open the popup and toggle the master shield OFF for a moment, or add an exemption in My Filters: @@||example.com^$document.
• Local UI (Open WebUI / App Hub) Blocked? Ensure you access via http://localhost:8081 or http://127.0.0.1:8890; our loopback rules automatically allow same-origin loopback requests.
• Filter Syntax Error? Lines with syntax errors are highlighted below the editor when clicking Apply & Compile.
